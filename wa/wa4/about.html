About

I'm a student of CU Boulder's ATLAS Institute and President of CU Game Development. I make games, game engines, and anything that requires a touch of creativity! I love sharing my knowledge and building community so if anything here piques your interest please send a message my way!

Outside of college I make stuff! I love to paint, woodwork, and make semi-useless devices with micro-controllers. Recently I designed, printed, and programmed a device that debubbles your soda! Combining all of these hobbies into a lovecraftian horror is my ultimate dream. If you wish to see that come to life, stick around here and something undoubtedly ridiculous is bound to pop up.

Like all Coloradans you can often find me out in the mountains hiking with my partner and climbing in the gym. At home I love to read and cuddle with my cute kitty Honey!

Check out my projects page for more about what I have worked on!
